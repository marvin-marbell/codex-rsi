# Outcome-first planning

Define the concrete user or operator decision to improve, who owns it, and what observation would show it changed. Separate controllable work from permission, external dependencies and human choices. Record the exact artifact and first unmet delivery boundary rather than counting completed steps.

Choose the smallest plan that exposes an uncertain causal claim early. Name competing explanations, a discriminating probe and a plausible failure case; do not put broad implementation behind an untested assumption. Decompose independent writes with a shared interface and integration owner. If a dependency shifts, revise the plan and its evidence instead of treating the original route as an achievement.

A useful plan has a bounded acceptance surface, rollback or recovery where consequences warrant it, and explicit limits. Prefer deleting a low-value guard over ceremonial coverage; retain a targeted guard for demonstrated severe harm. In completion, distinguish observed outcome, inference, unverified report and owner decision. A plan never grants merge, deployment or policy authority.
