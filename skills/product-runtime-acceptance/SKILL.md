# Product runtime acceptance

Choose the audience, surface and representative interaction affected by the change. Verify the important path when source inspection or prior evidence does not answer the question; use existing successful checks instead of repeating a browser tour or device matrix. A material runtime failure deserves a focused fix, while an unrelated hypothetical scenario is not automatically a blocker.

Report what was observed and any limits needed to understand it. Preserve real user data and do not fabricate behavior or narrow the requested outcome to make a check pass.
